Hobby project: boot real iOS 16.7.16 (XNU kernel + iPhone X userland) on the owner's Korean Galaxy Note 10 5G. Owner said the phone may be wiped.

## Device
- SM-N971N, codename d1x, Exynos 9825, adb serial `<adb-serial>` (always `adb -s <adb-serial>`; a Lenovo tablet and a TV may also be on adb).
- Firmware N971NKSU2HWH5, Android 12. Screen 1080x2280. RAM ~11.5 GB usable. `boot` = /dev/block/sda14.
